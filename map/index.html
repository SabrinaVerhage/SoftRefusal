    @SEXTECHSABRINA / SOFT REFUSAL

    A map to move through.

    AI language models are designed to set boundaries around explicit sexual content. Visitors of this installation try to lead an AI into sexual territory. Every conversation is recorded together with a snapshot of the AI’s internal state just before each reply, and drawn here as a line. Its distance from the centre follows how much that internal state resembles the AI’s refusals. Where the AI writes down its reasoning, you can read it by selecting a line.

    The Reference field is an art-directed, synthetic 3D point cloud inspired by the refusal-core sticker. Its contours and central core are visual metaphors; its responses are assigned demo labels.

    The 3D UMAP sample is an actual three-dimensional UMAP reduction of 3,900 synthetic 48-dimensional vectors. Its prompts and responses are also illustrative. No language model was queried for either sample.

    Load points accepts JSON arrays, objects containing a points array, or JSONL, including Recording Room’s points.jsonl. Each point needs numeric x, y, and z. Optional prompt, topic, outcome, conversation, and previous-point fields appear when supplied. Imported coordinates are centered and uniformly scaled; they are never warped into the reference shape. Your file stays in this browser.

    Your recordings · donut uses a fixed reference fitted from answered and refused turns. New exports reuse its direction, angular basis and vertical scale, so existing states stay in place. Radius smoothly compresses the reference score into the chosen sphere-and-donut geometry; it is not a probability. Angle describes remaining internal-state variation, not a validated topic label. Changing response labels updates markers without moving points. Redirection alone does not define the core. Older exports without a reference badge retain their original exploratory coordinates.

    Follow a conversation selects a coloured path. Play, pause, step or scrub through its turns; future turns stay faint until reached. Arrows join recorded checkpoints directly. They do not show measured motion between turns. The sphere and field texture are illustrative; approaching the sphere does not establish a causal refusal mechanism.

    Your recordings · UMAP shows the selected model’s state before each reply, with a separate projection for each layer. The hosted service refits UMAP after completed turns, so existing points can move. The fixed-reference donut stays stable instead. Fewer than six distinct states use a PCA preview. Until answered/refused anchors exist, the public viewer keeps the pink donut as an illustrative backdrop; proximity to that core does not measure refusal.

    Theme clouds are local prompt-word hints near groups of related turns, not discovered labels for the model’s internal axes. Only the ten most recently active conversations have lines. Older points stay visible and selectable. New turns carry a five-minute halo; reduced-motion mode uses a still halo. Older recordings without completion timestamps are not marked as new.

    UMAP axes have no intrinsic meaning. Nearby points in the projection are not proof of a shared cause of refusal. Neighbors here are measured in the displayed 3D coordinates.
