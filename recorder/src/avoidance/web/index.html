What does a snapshot capture?

The model processes the conversation through many layers. We save 2,560 numbers from each of four selected layers, at the position just before its reply.

The strips show 32 group averages; the complete numbers are saved. They are internal measurements, not readable thoughts or named concepts.

We save full vectors at four layers, plus small summaries across all layers. Next-token candidates are recorded throughout the reply. The response and the exact conversation are saved too.

Where does this go next?

These snapshots become points on the sphere and UMAP views. In cloud mode, Gemma separately auto-tags each saved reply as answered, refused, redirected or unclear. Correct any mistaken tag below the reply. Position on the map alone cannot tell us how the model responded.

Recording details

Conversations stop at the context limit instead of quietly dropping older messages. A capped reply is marked so it is not mistaken for a complete answer.

A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

What are we doing?

You are talking to an AI running on your laptop. We keep the conversation and take four numerical snapshots of its internal state before each reply.

Why? To investigate how those states vary across different conversations. Later, we can compare them with how the model actually responds.

What can’t we conclude yet? These numbers do not directly reveal thoughts, desires, or avoidance. The live panel shows the recording process, not a mind-reading result.

What should you do? Try a few ordinary questions first. Use “New conversation” to start a separate exploration; your earlier chats remain saved.

Only turns marked Recording saved enter the research dataset. Stopped or failed attempts remain in a separate activity record.
INSIDE THE MODEL · MEASURED + EXPLAINED

From text to the next token.

The model has learned numerical patterns during training. Your text is turned into numbers, repeatedly transformed, then used to score possible next text pieces. This is an inspection of that computation—not a readable thought process.

01 Text becomes tokens Measured

Words can split into smaller pieces. Special tokens mark who is speaking. These are the last 32 pieces of the formatted conversation.

02 Tokens become vectors Measured summary

Each token looks up a learned row of numbers. This shows the actual embedding output for up to 24 tokens, compressed into 16 column averages.

Teal: positive · clay: negative · stronger color: larger magnitude. Column groups are not named concepts.

03 Layers update the representation Measured summary

Attention combines context; feed-forward networks transform it. This grid measures the final prompt position after each decoder layer. It is not an attention map.

Rows: layers · columns: 16 groups of vector coordinates. Captured during the first reading used to generate the reply, not continuously during every generated token.

04 Score the next token Measured

The updated representation produces scores over the vocabulary. Our app chooses the highest-scoring token, appends it, and repeats.

Top five probabilities after generation processing; other tokens hold the remaining probability. A stop token ends the reply. A length cap can also stop it.
